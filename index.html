About Us

Borsch Club is a University of California, Berkeley student organization that unites all of those interested in exploring and connecting with East European communities. We hope to foster appreciation for diverse histories, cultures, and peoples by hosting various cultural, social, and academic activities throughout the year Come eat "борщ" with us! 🥘
